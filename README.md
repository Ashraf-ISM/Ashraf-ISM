## 🪪 About Me
I’m Md Ashraf, an M.Sc.(Tech) Applied Geophysics student at IIT(ISM) Dhanbad with hands-on experience in machine learning for geoscience, petrophysical analysis, and seismic data processing. My projects range from building a PyQt-based petrophysics GUI and developing facies classification models, to analyzing GPS data for co-seismic deformation. Skilled in Python, MATLAB, and industry-standard geoscience tools, I aim to integrate data-driven methods into exploration and hazard assessment workflows.,

- **Research experience** (earthquake studies, seismic data analysis, well log analysis)

## 🎯 Career Highlights
- **Petrophysical GUI Project** – Developed an interactive **PyQt-based GUI** for well log interpretation with LASIO and Plotly integration. Functions include porosity calculation, shale volume estimation, water saturation, and electrofacies visualization.
- **Summer Research Intern – IISc Bangalore**  
  GPS-based co-seismic deformation analysis of the **2015 Gorkha Earthquake** using GAMIT/GLOBK & GMT.
- **ONGC Internship – Cauvery Basin**  
  Involved in seismic acquisition, processing, VSP interpretation, and synthetic seismogram generation.
- **Petrophysical Case Study – Gorgonichthys-1 Well**  
  Estimated porosity, shale volume, water saturation; velocity analysis and lithofacies classification.

  Mapping, magnetics, resistivity tomography, GPR, HVSR, DC resistivity.
