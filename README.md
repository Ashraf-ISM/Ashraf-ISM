## 🎯 About Me

> *"Integrating AI with geoscience to unlock the secrets beneath the surface."*

🎓 **M.Sc.(Tech) Applied Geophysics** student at **IIT(ISM) Dhanbad**  
🔬 Specializing in **Machine Learning for Geoscience**, **Seismic Processing**, and **Seismology**  
📍 Based in **Dhanbad, Jharkhand, India**  

### 🚀 What I Do
- 🤖 **AI/ML in Geoscience**: Developing intelligent solutions for geological data interpretation
- 🌊 **Seismic Analysis**: Processing and interpreting seismic data for exploration and hazard assessment
- 📊 **Petrophysical Modeling**: Creating data-driven approaches for reservoir characterization
- 🌏 **Earthquake Studies**: Analyzing seismic patterns and co-seismic deformation
