## 🚀 **About Me**

*Transforming complex geophysical data into actionable subsurface intelligence.*

</div>

I'm **Md Ashraf**, an **Applied Geophysicist** with an M.Sc. (Tech.) in Applied Geophysics from **IIT (ISM) Dhanbad**. My interests lie at the intersection of geoscience, scientific computing, and artificial intelligence, with a focus on developing computational approaches to subsurface problems.

I work with geophysical datasets, combining domain knowledge with programming and data-driven methods to extract meaningful geological insights. My technical interests include seismic data processing and interpretation, petrophysical evaluation, well-log analysis, reservoir characterization, and machine learning applications in geoscience.

Beyond conventional geophysical analysis, I enjoy developing **Python-based scientific applications, predictive models, and automated analytical workflows**. My academic and technical journey includes research exposure at **IISc Bangalore** and technical experience at **ONGC**, along with projects involving seismic resolution enhancement, petrophysical interpretation, well-log machine learning, and earthquake data analytics.
